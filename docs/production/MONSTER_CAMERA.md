# Gridborn monster camera — profile-first visual bible

## Camera rule (approved)

**Nonhumanoid monsters** (all quadrupeds, beasts, reptilian monsters,
dragons, spiders/insects, oozes, aberrations, and complex flying creatures)
must be illustrated and animated in **side view or three-quarter side view**.
Expose the full body length, species-defining jaws/snout, tail, wings, number
of limbs, and natural attack direction.

**Upright humanoid monsters** (orcs, goblins, skeleton warriors, zombies,
cultists, soldiers, giants, similar humanoid-bodied undead/constructs) may
retain **three-quarter/front-facing views** because these communicate their
gear, armor, face, and weapons better. No arbitrary rule makes all undead
front-facing: quadrupedal undead are side-view creatures.

The full 330-creature index records the *target art direction only*. This
metadata never certifies the current art image. In particular, the existing
Spider artwork is an earlier, predominantly frontal study and must be
revised before passing the silhouette review. The existing Wolf is a
three-quarter side study and is closer to the desired standard.

## Pose and motion acceptance

- Profile read at 256, 128, 64, **32 px** on a light/dark 5-foot grid.
- Creature shape first; eyes, anatomy, tail, legs, and wings second.
- Idle: visible natural motion, seamless.
- Move: species-specific weight shift and correctly grounded limb movement.
- Attack: one anticipation, one clearly directed strike, recovery once.
- Rest/sleep: unique natural anatomy; no seated human pose on a spider,
  dog, ooze, or dragon.
- Mirroring: no text, directional magical glyph or asymmetric branded
  design that would break when token is mirrored.
- Outlines, padding, alpha edges and perspective consistent with Gridborn.
- Only mark completed when human review and actual VTT import certification
  pass. Metadata alone must not satisfy any release gate.

## NPC and hero expansion

The browse roster adds **108 class hero concepts**, exactly three male,
three female and three nonbinary per class (nine per class × twelve classes).
These are original fictional character identities. Gender does not prescribe
skin tone, hairstyle, silhouette or equipment. Individual designs should
differ through class gear, hairstyle, ancestry, palette and character details,
without relying on gender stereotypes.

The browse roster also adds **48 NPC concepts** spanning twelve occupations
and four ancestries. Both rosters are **production queues**. Concept cards
with no illustration explicitly show *Artwork pending* and have no purchase
path. The character builder can load the 108 variant recipes but these
are not yet 108 original portrait illustrations or complete animation exports.
