## Independent storyboard and role-specific action
- **Idle (3.2 sec):** performance breathing and plucking-hand micro-gesture while lute stays cradled.
- **Move (2.4 sec clip):** alternate short steps; body, lute and both arms translate together; restrained moving string contact.
- **Attack (1.4 sec once):** brace, strum an accented chord with pivot at soundboard, brighten strings, independently launch a ribbon of gold/teal harmonic arcs toward screen-right from the soundboard. The magic effect is separate from the instrument rig, fades and recovers; not a fake waving lute.
- **Rest (4.0 sec):** instrument lowers and halfling posture softens, without spell effects.
